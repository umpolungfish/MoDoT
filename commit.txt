The word instruments move into imasm_core, and ask grows a word lane

ask had seventy-one flags and every one of them reads a CATALOG ENTRY — a name,
a tuple. The kernel's cycle, weight, banked, trans, insert and ringspec read an
IMASM WORD, and they lived only in the kernel REPL, so reaching them meant
booting mOMonadOS and driving it over a serial script. That is what I did all
night to run ROTAT on a key word, and it is why the word lane was missing from
every analysis until I went around it by hand.

They are not ported and not copied. They MOVE, into imasm_core, which both this
crate and the kernel already depend on. ringspec needed nothing but alloc.
lattice_flow needed imasm16_3, which is in the same crate, plus the kernel's
print macro — so the reports build a String now and the kernel prints what they
return. The `sw!` shim is what made that mechanical rather than a rewrite.

What stayed behind is the part that is genuinely the kernel's: insert_sweep_all
walks THIS kernel's program table, which the shared crate has no notion of. It
lives in mOMonadOS beside a wrapper that prints the shared reports.

New in ask: --ringspec, --rotat, --weight, --banked, --trans, --insert. The
orbit verb is --rotat rather than --cycle because --cycle is already the
catalytic loop here; two different objects must not share a flag.

PHASE-BEARING with 2 distinct landings, and --weight and --banked agree with the
kernel line for line.
