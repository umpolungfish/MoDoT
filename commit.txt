The trilattice table was still on the old marks

`classic.rs` moved to the new alphabet and `imasm16_3.rs` did not, so the two
faces of one language disagreed about what a glyph is. It showed up in the kernel
rather than here: `cycle ⊢⊙∈⊤⊥∋⋈◻⊣` came back as ⊢⊙∈∋⊣, five tokens out of nine,
because `parse_glyph_word` matches against the tri table and ⊤ ⊥ ⋈ ◻ were not in
it. Unknown glyphs are dropped, not refused, so the word was silently read as a
shorter word and judged as one.

Both tables carry the same twelve now, and the tri parser takes the retired
spellings too: ◇ ● ☊ ☋ for the dyad, and = + × ¬ for the four that carried
equality, sum, product and negation. A word in either spelling loads and prints
in the new one.
