Check the sharded decoder against candle's, and the 3.5 chat template against our engine

IG_LOCAL_FORCE_SPLIT runs the sharded stack on a single card. It buys nothing at
runtime; it exists so the two decoders can be compared on the SAME card, where
identical greedy output means identical arithmetic rather than a coincidence of
two architectures. Qwen3-1.7B, temperature 0, 476 tokens: byte-identical.

Two tests render a model's own chat template through the same minijinja
environment the engine builds, so what a template needs is measured rather than
assumed. Qwen3.5's template renders here — the reverse walk over messages, the
namespaces, the macro and the pycompat string methods all hold — and a multi-turn
history drops the earlier turn's reasoning and opens a think block on the live
turn, which is the behaviour the model card asks for.
