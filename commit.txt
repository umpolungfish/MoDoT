Follow the model's own card, and refuse a hybrid instead of answering wrongly

Sampling was one global default wearing Qwen3's numbers. It now comes from the
card of the model actually loaded: Qwen3 thinks at 0.6 with a repetition penalty
of 1.15 and 8192 tokens of output, Qwen3.5 at 1.0 with a presence penalty of 1.5,
no repetition penalty, and 32768 tokens. The presence penalty is implemented
properly — a constant subtracted once from every distinct token already emitted —
rather than folded into the repetition penalty, which divides and fires on every
repeat. They are different operations and a card asking for one is not asking for
the other. Qwen3's own path is unchanged: the same question at temperature 0
gives the same 476 tokens it gave before.

The loader now reads a nested text_config and carries the tensor prefix through
to the sharded stack, so a checkpoint whose text stack lives under
model.language_model. is addressable. And it refuses what it cannot run, by name:
Qwen3.5 is a hybrid whose linear_attention layers are gated DeltaNet with a
conv1d and a recurrent state, its rotary is partial and multimodal, and reading
those weights as attention would not fail — it would produce noise. The message
counts the layers and points at vLLM or SGLang. Tested against the 3.5-4B on
disk: 24 of 32 layers named, and the refusal is what comes back.

Its chat template needs nothing: it renders through the engine's minijinja as
written, drops the earlier turn's reasoning in a multi-turn history, and opens
the think block the model expects.
