Split only when the split buys something

The first cut split a model across both cards whenever both were open, which
made a 1.7B that fits either card alone run at 7 tok/s instead of 20 — the
partition costs a device-to-device copy per forward and it should only be paid
when it buys VRAM. Now an auto-detected pair splits only when the weights plus a
working reserve will not sit on the roomiest card alone, and a pair named in
IG_DEVICES splits because that is a decision. When one card is enough it is the
roomiest card that takes the model, not whichever ordinal came first.
