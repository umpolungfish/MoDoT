ask: the ob3ect designer was capped, and the cap read as an empty answer

8192 tokens on the design call, against a reasoning model that spends its budget
on reasoning before the answer starts. What comes back is content: "" with no
error attached, reported as "empty content" — a failure that names nothing.

Three changes. max_tokens = 0 now means uncapped and the field leaves the payload
entirely; the ob3ect path passes 0, since a design call is never capped. And when
content is empty the error says how many characters of reasoning came back and
what finish_reason was, so the next person sees the budget rather than a mystery.
