Colour reaches the surface people actually read

The styling went onto the tool verbs and stopped there, so the path a normal run
takes — the ManuscriptSpine banners, the section rules, the outer spine — stayed
exactly as plain as before. Which is why nothing looked different: the coloured
part was the part you only see when you call click or forge directly.

Both spine banners now use the same header as every verb, the twenty-three
`── SECTION ──` headings become real rules, and the univocal verdict — the one
line a reader is actually looking for — is bold with its B4 letter painted by
what it says. The bare 60-character `=` and `═` bars are gone in favour of the
shared rule, so the output has one visual vocabulary instead of three.

Still off entirely when stdout is not a terminal.
