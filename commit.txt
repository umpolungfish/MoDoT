AREV is its own two factors, and the flow gate was carrying them

TNEG and INEG are not a thirteenth and fourteenth operator. The involution swaps
big_t with big_f AND small_t with small_f; TNEG is the first swap alone and INEG
the second alone, on disjoint bits, so they commute and composing them IS AREV.
On the classical slice INEG has no bits to move, so AREV is TNEG there. That is
the same shape as the dyad: one operator, and the carrier says how much of it
there is to see.

The flow evaluator implemented neither. `gate_out` had a case for AREV and none
for the two swaps, so they fell through to carry, and `⊢~⊣` returned its input
untouched while claiming to be a WORK opcode. Both are gates now, built from two
new register projections that name the layers they move.

Measured: seeded Tf, AREV gives Ft, TNEG gives Ff, INEG gives Tt, and either
order of the two gives Ft. On the classical slice seeded T, AREV and TNEG both
give F and INEG gives T. The identity holds in the machine, not just in the
register type.

This also settles what the twelve-opcode core is doing without them: nothing is
missing from it. Whatever slot 4 is spelled with carries the whole symmetry of
the carrier, and the two swaps are that symmetry restricted to one layer at a
time.
