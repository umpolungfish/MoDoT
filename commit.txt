The tuple keys move, and the type lookup finds its own ob3ects again

`TUPLE_ORDER` in imasm.rs and `PRIM_KEYS` in main.rs are the two places this tool
indexes a catalog entry by axis, and both are on the new primitive alphabet. The
parse of the ⟨…⟩ notation is positional and unaffected; values did not move.

Found while checking the round trip: `expand_type` looked for the 49 primitive
type ob3ects under ~/imsgct/MoDoT/ob3ects, a directory that stopped existing when
MoDoT became 2m3iosis, so every `imasm expand` and every `imasm cycle` answered
"type not found" for all 49 rather than saying the root was gone. The tree's own
path is tried first now, with the old ones kept behind it.

With that fixed the round trip runs: `imasm cycle tuple=⟨𐑦𐑸𐑾𐑹𐑐𐑧𐑲𐑠⊙𐑫𐑳𐑭⟩`
recovers 12 of 12 axes exactly, the monad among them.
