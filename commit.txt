A verdict is two bits off the end of a word, not a reading of it

The agent was told to type-check its thinking and then given nothing to do with
the answer. So a word arrived, returned T or B, and that was the whole
interrogation — while rotat, weight, banked, trans, insert and ringspec sat unused
and every one of them says something the verdict cannot.

Now a hard rule: every IMASM word produced or received — the agent's own
type-check, a lifted proof term, a key's address, a monomer's composition — is
CYCLED and then interrogated, and what the instruments returned is reported rather
than the verdict alone.

Each is named with what it is FOR, since a list of verbs teaches nothing: rotat
reads the landing register at every cut, because the verdict and topology hold
across the orbit while the final register does not, which makes the phase the only
handle on where a word rests; weight reads what each step carries and what
survives; banked asks whether a clear ever fires against a live register with
nothing behind it, and says outright that VACUOUS is a real answer rather than a
failure; trans counts transitions ON THE RING including the closing edge, which a
linear reading cannot see; insert is the repair.

And the reason, stated so it is not read as ceremony: two words with the same
verdict routinely differ in every one of these, and the difference is usually the
finding.
