ask: add --meet, the operation that descends

Every entity-to-entity verb in this binary RAISES. preclick says it outright -- "the blend
is LOSSY (max per axis)" -- and click, fuse, cycle, set and polymerize all take the max.
--anneal lowers only a ring's strain; --descend only ⊙ and a winding. So a walk that needs
a slot brought DOWN could not be performed at all, and the ones that need it are not
exotic: ⊢ 𐑛, ≻ 𐑑 and ∋ 𐑝 toward CLINK L9, ∈ ℵ→ℷ toward L8.

That gap is why the perfect_cuboid walk to L9 runs 62 catalog entries. Every lowering had
to be smuggled through side effects -- Coagula imprints, catalyst turnover -- rather than
performed, and its ring_promoted through ring_promoted8 all DESCRIBE "⊥ promoted 𐑖→𐑫"
while their stored tuples keep 𐑖. The promotion kept being undone by the next blend.

  --meet A B [--certify] [--register [NAME]]

The greatest lower bound, min per axis: what the two entities SHARE, not what they fuse
to. It reports which side supplied each floor. Where a slot is absent on either side the
meet is absent too -- an unknown floor is not a floor. --certify runs the same Lean
kernel gate as --click; --register canonizes it the same way.
