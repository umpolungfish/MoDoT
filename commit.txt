Every mOMonadOS verb is reachable from ask

The kernel carries well over two hundred verbs. Porting them one at a time
leaves the set incomplete for as long as the porting takes, and the requirement
is availability, so `--kernel <verb> [args…]` bridges all of them at once.

It drives the HOSTED build over stdin rather than the serial script: no QEMU, no
fixed sleeps, no boot image. Everything before the first prompt is the kernel
starting up — catalog load, ordinal check, Frobenius identity, banner — and
belongs to the boot rather than the answer, so it is cut. `--kernel help` lists
the verbs. MOMONADOS_BIN overrides the path.

This does not retire the relocation route. A verb that is pure computation should
still MOVE into imasm_core the way ringspec and lattice_flow just did, so it needs
no kernel at all and no subprocess; the bridge is what makes it available in the
meantime, and what covers the verbs that genuinely need the kernel's own state.

Checked through the bridge: ringspec, straus, sigma.
